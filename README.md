# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple and efficient tool to calculate simple interest based on principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period in years.

## Formula Used

The formula for calculating simple interest is:

$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount (Anapara)
- **R** = Rate of interest per annum (Yıllık Faiz Oranı %)
- **T** = Time period in years (Zaman - Yıl)

## Features

- Quick calculation of simple interest.
- Easy to use and clear structure.
- Open-source and licensed under Apache 2.0.

## Usage Example

If you invest **$1,000** at an annual interest rate of **5%** for **3 years**:

- **P** = 1000
- **R** = 5
- **T** = 3

$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{1000 \times 5 \times 3}{100} = 150$$

Total Amount = $1,000 + $150 = **$1,150**
